# Coauthor review

Open https://eitt.github.io/spc-coauthor-review/ and choose your task.

Each tab contains the question to review and a response box. Choose **Confirmed**, **Change needed**, or **Not assessed**, write your observations, then use **Copy response** and paste the result into the corresponding Word comment. Product-pair review has one decision and note per pair.

Draft responses stay in the current browser. They are not sent to a server. Download your responses to keep a copy; use the same browser to continue. No response has been approved or submitted automatically.

The scientific record contains 34 pairs, 102 scenarios, 274 physical attempts and 269 valid final responses. Five timeouts and two skipped second passes remain included. Historical results are not regenerated by the page.

The original study repository remains private. Only the authorized, cleaned review materials are in this repository. Full session histories, credentials, local paths and source workbooks are excluded. The evidence ZIP is a separate public subset. Historical office files and the full comment history stay in the local private archive; use SPC_manuscript_review.docx for current review.

AGRIBALYSE 3.2 attribution and source links are in the evidence manifest. Database indicators are not a new LCA or a comprehensive sustainability assessment. No new license is asserted over third-party source material or the manuscript.
